About me

I started on the structural side, drafting in AutoCAD and modeling in ETABS, working through RC frame design on real residential and industrial projects as a Junior Civil Engineer. That hands-on exposure still shapes how I approach a project: load paths, constructability, and where the budget actually goes in a design. From there, my path shifted into cost control and estimation, where I leaned into automation, building tools that take the manual grind out of take-offs, budgeting, and reporting. I'm genuinely happy doing cost work today, but structural design is where I started, and it's a door I'd gladly walk back through given the right opportunity.
